# Roadmap — Wordbound: Crescendo

North star, set 2026-08-21: build Jaxon's "words vs music" game to a playable,
impressive vertical slice, then grow it to launch quality. This is a sibling game to
Wordbound (repo: descent-of-essence, engine forked at its v0.42 state, 2026-08-21) —
the engine is expected to DIVERGE here; no compatibility obligation in either
direction. Wordbound's own itch.io launch continues separately in its own repo.

## The pitch

Word-battler vs. living classical music. Spell words to fight musical enemies; bosses
are backed by famous operas/classical pieces and ATTACK ON THE CRESCENDOS — you play
under time pressure, going for the best word you can get NOW, and a word landed right
on the crescendo parries the hit. Permanent progression: the enemy faction stole the
alphabet; win your letters back run by run.

## Milestones

1. **Vertical slice** (the current GOALS.md queue through the crescendo-combat
   ticket): one fully playable boss fight with sequenced music, crescendo attacks,
   parry, entrance cutscene. This is the "is it fun?" checkpoint — Jaxon playtests
   here before the roster gets built out.
2. **Full loop**: stolen-letters meta, item batch, regular-enemy roster.
3. **Launch-readiness**: polish/QA passes, itch build, store copy (reuse the sibling
   repo's pipeline patterns).
